# The author's corrections

What the author has corrected an agent on, in the author's words and as a
rule. Read it with [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md). **The latest word wins:** a new
correction that contradicts an entry replaces it, and the older entry is
removed, never kept beside it. Newest first.

## 2026-10-06

- **"Clean it to the bar" is entropy, not style.** "AGENT.md랑 common.c라고
  말하면 엔트로피라는 말이 저절로 안 떠오름?" Pointed at AGENTS.md or
  calab-loader, cleaning means one owner a fact, copies derived or deleted,
  behavior changing where two owners disagreed. Rewording comments while two
  owners remain is not cleaning. Under an unclear scope, take what AGENTS.md
  requires, never the smallest diff.
- **Overhaul, don't append.** "중축하지 마시고 … 쓸데없는 찌꺼기 쌓여있으면
  좀 갈아엎으세요." A correction to code or a document is folded into what is
  there; an added paragraph that restates or patches is a second owner.
- **Corrections live here, linked, not in AGENTS.md or an agent's memory.**
  "그걸 에이전트 md에 왜 처박아. 따로 링크 걸어서 모아놔." "메모리에 남기지 말고."

## 2026-10-05

- **Build once, when the whole change is done; the author looks.** "리빌드
  자체를 하지 말라고. 수정이나 해. 완전히, 다 끝나서 딱 한번 돌리라고."
  "확인은 내가 하니까 토큰 까먹지 말고." No compile, test, lint or rebuild
  between edits, and no browser driving to check a screen unless asked.
- **Done means done.** "자연스럽다니까." When the author says it looks right,
  stop auditing it.
- **A request names its scope, and all of it is done.** "구름은 무죄임 …
  구름 건드리면 뒤진다", then "다 손대라고." What is not named is left
  alone; what is named, a reviewer's list handed over included, is all done.
- **Cross-checks cover every path to the symptom.** "상쇄시키랬더니 왜 로컬
  근처에서 애무를 해." A review that checks the diff's neighborhood and calls
  it sound has not reviewed.
- **Answer the question asked.** "말귀를 왜 못 알아듣지?" "뭘로 안 보인다고
  한건지 물어보잖아." Answer what was asked, from what was actually looked at,
  and say when a claim was not checked.
- **Remove the cause, not the symptom's neighbor.** "걍 줄을 없애면 안돼? 저기
  처리가 왜 되어야 하는건데?" Before tuning what produces an artifact, ask
  whether that computation needs to exist; two computations of one quantity
  meeting at a seam are the defect.
