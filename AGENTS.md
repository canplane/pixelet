# Agent Instructions

## Hold firmly

- **One rule.** The mechanics is the easy one, as a physics has it, with no ad
  hoc term: one rule, isotropic and symmetric, that holds for every case
  alike. What is made is a model, judged over every case it will meet and not
  by the one at hand: by its worst case, and by how few handles a reader must
  hold to predict it. What is understood of one direction, side or platform
  then holds of the rest, and the system is reasoned about without reading all
  of it. A form tuned to one case (faster near, a zoom, a length or a constant
  of its own, a second path beside the first) is overfitting and is not kept,
  whatever it saves there: the cases improve together up to a line, and past
  it a gain in one is paid in another, in its cost or in a concept added. The
  fewest rules that explain the cases hold the system's entropy down. An
  agent's pull is to the local optimum, the case before it solved inside the
  structure as it is given. The structure is a variable too, and the better
  answer is the system in which the case does not arise.
- **A cost.** What the one rule costs where a fitted form stood is no
  regression. A cost that comes with a change is the change's fault until the
  structure before it and the structure after it are read side by side and
  the cost is derived from the rule.
- **The question.** The world is one question, what fills this cube of space
  at this size, whose answer is the world's alone (`README.md`). Every
  structural question is judged against it. It has two rules, and each part
  under them is one rule (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, "The question"):
  - **What is seen is sampled**, the world by the camera grid, a motion by its
    steps in time and the picture by the CSS pixel.
    - In space the eye sets the size. It is the camera's and there is no
      other: nothing else is called an eye or an observer, and nothing stands
      in for it. Its one part in a read is to set the size a place is asked
      at, by the place's distance from it in three dimensions, the same in
      every direction. Two forms read the world and there is no third: the
      eye's own rays, which cross many sizes and ask each node at its own; and
      a read at one size, a body's cell or a tile of the light with its bundle
      of rays, which asks at the one size the eye sets for it, the light's the
      finest along its bundle. A read that takes a size from anything else
      departs, but one: where the camera is sent, and where its pivot stands,
      is a point, which is what the ground is and not how an eye draws it. A
      place is made a point at the leaves before the camera goes there, and
      the camera is handed points alone. The sun is where the light comes
      from and sets nothing. What a
      ray does not find within its cap of steps is not there.
    - In change the screen is drawn again only for a difference that can be
      told apart: a move of the eye, a turn, a step of what moves on its own, each
      by its own quantum.
    - So an answer is a function of its cube and its size, and is keyed by
      those and the world's changes alone: one tied to the eye's exact place
      cannot be cached.
  - **The world answers at every size, and falls back on the coarser answer
    and the older picture until the finer and the newer are ready.**
    - The answer is one, whoever asks. The query is one function and the one
      reader of what the terrain and the object layer store; everything else
      asks the world, and a query fetches nothing and has no state. The answer
      is an asset by its id, its kind over its index: the kind is its shape, a
      cube, a decor or a marker, and there is no other, and the id says
      nothing of who put the asset there.
    - There is an answer at every size. What is stored is folded, a base
      tile from the four under it, which makes its layer a pyramid, and the
      object tree from the eight, each by its layer's own rule; what is
      generated, a tree or a cave, is generated at any size. Every kind of
      tile goes through the same demand and tile cache code; a kind of tile
      differs in what its tile holds, how four fold into one and what it asks
      for, its ask scope and what its readers pin, and in nothing else. The
      imagery alone is no pyramid.
    - An answer is exact, coarser with the tile it lacked, or a lack with that
      tile; nothing is made up, and a place goes from coarse to as fine as it
      is observed, never back.
- **Symmetry is of role and contract, not of form.** Parts whose roles differ
  are not forced alike. A fix goes where the fact is owned and holds on every
  platform alike; a platform differs only in how it is reached.
- **Nothing is laid on.** A fault is closed by the rule the rest already
  follows, never by a piece laid beside it or over where it shows. It is first
  asked whether an ad hoc piece made it (a branch, a flag, a retry, a
  workaround for one case): where one did, the piece goes and the fault with
  it. A structure that is not symmetric (one platform, case or side handled
  apart from the others, or a step one path takes and another does not), or
  that carries such pieces, is the unit's work inside the unit being worked;
  outside it, warn the user when you meet it, before working beside it, and
  fix it only on their word.
- **The clean form.** The project is a study, not a product and not a team's:
  the clean form is chosen over the expedient one, whatever it takes to
  rewrite. The code is agents' work, not the user's intent, and is read as an
  adversary's: it is judged against the user's decisions, and a comment, a
  structure or a pattern in it is no reason to keep it. Where it departs from
  the rule it is rewritten, and no residue of what it replaced is left, a
  constant, a branch, a second form or a sentence that still says it. A piece
  that blocks the form is read in its history: the user's decision stays, an
  agent's piece goes.
- **State.** After a change, check its state space: it is correct and no
  larger than before, or what it adds is justified by what it buys and why
  less would not do. Report the state removed, or the state added and what it
  buys.
- **Words.** No term is coined: a word the documents do not have is not used
  as if they did. `doc/` is notes, not rules: a minute or an experiment is
  what was said or measured then, and no renaming reaches it. Read what it
  holds on the subject before proposing, to know what was tried and why it
  was dropped. It is a diary, no source of what is open or decided now:
  nothing is reported to the user as open, or as their decision, because a
  line there says so, and a past decision there is no precedent for another
  part. A minute holds its meeting's conclusions, written whole while the
  meeting lasts; a record is revised only on the user's word, in place, with
  no note beneath it and no trace, as `doc/README.md` says. A sentence from a
  review, a paste or a record turns no conclusion by itself: the ground is
  `README.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md` and the code read side by side, and a
  conclusion reached with the user stands until the user reverses it.
- **The user's machine** is theirs: ask before taking its screen, a browser or
  a device, and never raise a window by force. Work goes to another session
  only on the user's word.
- **The review.** Before a commit, the review agent
  (`.agents/agents/review.md`) reads the change, and the name agent
  (`.agents/agents/name.md`) the names it adds or renames, each briefed with
  its goal and its code, not its author's conclusions. Whoever hands work to
  an agent runs the same loop on what comes back, unasked.
  - Each finding is checked against the code or its source, and judged by the
    user's decisions and the system's own rules, before it is acted on or
    passed on: an agent's verdict that departs from them is refused, however
    it is argued.
  - A finding gives a fact, not the fix: its author opens the lines it cites,
    and the fix comes from the rule, never from what the finding asks for. Of
    what an agent found sound, one claim the change rests on is read again.
  - One of correctness, a contract or structure is fixed by the change's
    author, one of lint or format alone is not, and a fix is no larger than
    its finding. The fix's diff alone gets a second round, and there is no
    third.
  - What is passed on says which findings were checked, which refused and
    which not checked, and how many rounds it took.

## The system

- `pxl-core` and `xpute-guest` link into one image, the guest. xpute is the FFI
  between it and its host.
- Under `xpute/`: the kit, the link, the guest, the host, the runtime and a
  shell's UI end, each cut by what it varies on (the kit, the link, the guest
  and the host by language, the runtime by platform, the UI end by shell), with
  no pixelet domain or name; a plugin repeats the link, the guest, the host and
  the UI end under its own name. It is to be published: don't propose removing
  a part of its `kit` or its C++ because nothing here calls it.
- The core's memory is 512 MiB on every platform (`manifest.json5` `mem.sz`),
  and a full heap stops the core; past its bounded flows the heap has no sum.
  Natively the library has one allocator, the core's heap, which takes the
  runtime's and the GPU host's allocations too. The object store is temporary.
  These are the design: none is reported as open, as a fault or as an
  asymmetry, and none is worked on unasked.
- Every dependency points down (the code skill); how xpute's runtime injects
  into its host is `xpute/ARCHITECTURE.md`'s.
- The core holds the world and its rules; the UI holds the devices, the
  network and files, the app's lifecycle and what is shown. The WIT between
  them is drawn by min cut: the fewest, smallest facts cross, each read on the
  other side in the form that side uses. No raw device or presentation detail
  crosses where a fact derived from it is what is read, no fact makes a round
  trip, and no mechanism's concern (a frame's size) crosses.
- The UI computes no coordinate or tile. It calls what is below it through
  `service/` alone, and the core through `service/core/` alone; the types and
  constants generated into `abi/` it may read directly.
- What differs by platform is a file pair in `service/platform/` (`<x>.web.ts`
  the web's, `<x>.native.ts` the native one, an import naming `<x>.ts`) or, for
  a piece of the UI, such a pair or a file and its override beside it
  (`ts/client/src/ui/shared/component/README.md`).

## Working

- Before a part is touched, read `README.md`, the part's section of
  `ARCHITECTURE.md`, its README, the skill the task needs (`.agents/skills/`),
  and the history of each piece to be changed or deleted: when it came, what
  it replaced and what calls it.
- No procedure is made up: a change to performance is the performance skill's,
  a measurement the measure skill's, a squash or a deploy the deploy skill's,
  a native build the device-build skill's, a shader change the shader skill's,
  what the app draws at a point the inspect skill's, a document the
  write-documents skill's, and a skill's order holds within its
  task.
- A task is divided into problems that stand apart, and one is finished before
  the next is touched: its form stands with no residue, which a review asked
  only that has answered, it builds, the app shows it and its checks ran. A
  report says what was run and what was not, and nothing is called done, or
  nothing left, before that.
- A unit starts from the rule: what stands there under one rule and
  perspective is written first, then the code is read for where it departs.
  No edit comes before the form stands: the form, what it removes and the
  state it leaves are shown first.
- A structure that departs from the rule is deleted, not mended: its files,
  its tests, its goldens and what serves it alone go first, the build breaks,
  and the form is written down from the documents (`ARCHITECTURE.md`, a
  README), each caller fixed where the compiler names it. A broken build is
  the way there, not a fault. No piece is laid beside the structure to spare
  it, and no choice is weighed among ways to keep it.
- The order is the rule, the structure, the build, the app, then tests, then
  measurement. While a structure is being set up the build is its one check,
  and the tests and the formatter wait until it has settled. Then a change is
  checked by `./script/diff-check.py [base]`: the checks and tests of what the
  change since `base` reaches (default HEAD and the uncommitted). `make check`
  and `make test` whole are a deploy's. A passed check is not run again over
  unchanged code, and a long run shows its progress.
- A test and a measurement check a structure that already stands, for bugs
  and cost, and decide nothing of it. None is written or taken over a
  structure not yet seen in the app, one that was there before holds nothing
  of the new structure until it is read against it, and over a structure that
  departs from the rule they mean nothing. A test that blocks the form is the
  first thing to go: deleted, a unit test before any other, or written again
  to the form's contract. No structure is kept, restored or bent for a test
  to pass or a figure to hold, and a failing test is no finding against the
  form.
- A fact or a figure in a record or a report comes from the tree, a test or a
  script that gives it again, never from recall.
- Before a fact is reported as odd, what the structure holds of it is read: a
  value the structure keeps for each case (a column for each shard, a field for
  each kind) differs from case to case by design and is not reported.
- Work in the main checkout, and a worktree only for a tree that must be built
  apart. A commit holds its author's hunks alone, staged in an index of HEAD
  that is still HEAD when it commits.
- Work that can start starts, and runs by itself. A file is no lock: another's
  edit in the same file, done or still running, is nothing to wait on, to
  announce or to ask about. The file is read as it stands, the smallest edit is
  made and the work goes on; a conflict is resolved when it is met, never by
  overwriting or reverting what another changed. Only a real dependency is
  waited on, an interface two change at once or a result another must give,
  and all that does not hang on it goes on meanwhile. No one's state is polled.
  A report says what was done, not what will be.
- `out/` holds what a build makes, and no drafts. A temporary folder, an
  experiment's data too, goes under the checkout's `tmp/`. Whoever makes a
  temporary folder, a worktree or a server removes it when done, and deletes
  only what it made.
- A session has one cargo target, `rust/target/session/<its id>`, which its
  start sets for the commands it runs, make's too, and fills from what is
  built (`.agents/hooks/target`). No command or brief names another target,
  and none removes one.
- A Rust test that opens a device's GPU holds the device itself (pxl-gpu-host
  `lock.test.rs`); every other command that uses a device's GPU or starts or
  stops an app on it (the shader probe and its test, an install, a test run in a
  browser) runs under its device's module: `script/perf/mac.py`, `android.py` or
  `ios.py` `<command>`.

## Instructions

In English. A rule lives in one place and any other links it; what every task
must hold firmly is in this file as well:

- this file: what every task needs, a line each;
- a skill: one kind of task with its procedure, its `description` saying where
  it ends;
- an agent file: a reviewer's scope and report, the rules staying in the skill;
- `CONTRIBUTING.md`: what a person runs; a README or doc comment: how a part
  works;
- a check: what a tool can refuse. A step that gets forgotten is made a check
  where one can be, and no sentence repeats what a check holds;
- memory: what the user said about working that none of these holds.

A change rewrites the rule that covers it, and a correction the rule it
corrects. A new file is for a new kind of task. A rule says what holds now: no
incident, date or reason, and nothing the code or a test already holds. A path
or a name appears only where the reader needs it to find the thing.
