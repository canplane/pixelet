# The record

What the system is lives in the code, `ARCHITECTURE.md` and the READMEs
beside the code: they are what binds today. This folder holds
how it came to be that way and what is next, the way a company keeps its
agenda, its minutes and its reports beside its rules.

- [backlog.md](./backlog.md) — the agenda: what is queued, in priority
  order, one line each, pointing at where its reasoning lives.
- [minutes/](./minutes/) — one meeting's record a file,
  `YYYYMMDD-topic.md`: what was asked, what was weighed, and what was
  resolved, if anything was. A design or a report written for a meeting
  becomes its minutes once the meeting is over. Minutes that still carry
  weight are kept up and may be edited.
- [minutes/archive/](./minutes/archive/) — minutes no longer read for
  facts. They move here on the user's word, whether what they resolved is
  now carried by the code and the rules or was discarded; success and
  failure retire alike. Archived minutes are never a ground for a judgment
  now, and something at odds with them is no contradiction.
- [experiment/](./experiment/) — measurements, dated, each with its
  procedure, its numbers and what produced them.

A resolution binds once the code or `ARCHITECTURE.md` carries it or cites
it; where one of them and the minutes disagree, they win.
Minutes that replace an earlier resolution say which.

The record is history, and its links and names are not tracked: one found
stale is fixed when seen, and only a broken one that misleads is worth a
change of its own.

A report or a request from outside the project is a GitHub issue. What is
resolved in one is written here, and the issue links to it.
