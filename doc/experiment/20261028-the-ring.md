# The ring

**2026-10-28.** E4 of the grazing rows, after
[the cone touches](./20261028-the-cone-touches.md) failed on time. The
prune stays on, exactly as HEAD's. Where a sample stops, the cells about
the cell it stopped at are brought in at its zoom, and the filled ones are
handed to the raster as boxes of their own at their true size. The aim is
to mitigate the rows, not to remove them at any cost. A hole shows through
only where three cells in a row are missed: the stopped one, and a
neighbour on each side.

**Rejected.** The ring took the walks again down to a third. Seen on the
screen, though, with its boxes deduplicated, the user found the rows still
there and the look bad. Its raster still cost about a millisecond of GPU p95
looking down with the shadows on (second run, below). It exists only to make
up for the prune, so once the prune goes it has nothing left to do.

## Why

E3 showed that a touch rule costs more than the frame has. This one keeps
the prune and its cost as they are, and spends a little on the raster to
fill the rows from beside the cells the samples did stop at.

## How

An export of `d8f712deb` on :5178, against :5176 (HEAD's code). The patch
(580 lines) is kept outside the tree, at the session's scratchpad
`e4.patch`.

- **The ring** (pxl-shader world.rs `World::ringed`).
  - All 26 cells about the stop, at its zoom, are asked once a box. Each is
    asked off the stop's line (`support::asset`), as `faced` asks its own.
  - The face word is then read off those answers, so nothing `faced` asked
    is asked twice.
  - Today a box asks at most 12, lazily (all 12 on flat ground). The ring
    asks 26, so it adds 14 or more a box.
- **Its boxes** (pxl-shader raster.rs `face_cs`).
  - Each filled ring cell becomes a box at its drawn size (`cast::drawn`),
    drawn across its sample's axis.
  - A ring cell is skipped where the cell in front of it across that axis
    is whole: the ring's, or the stop itself. It can never show past it,
    and no ask is added to learn this.
  - A ring box carries its own asset and zoom in the box's words
    (`raster::BOX_ASSET`, `BOX_ZOOM`). Its number is its place among the
    boxes, marked `raster::RING`.
  - It takes its stop's looks where its asset matches, else none. It takes
    no face word, so it shows no outline.
- **The pick is today's** (pxl-shader cast.rs `cast_cs`): the ray's exact
  entry, refused across any other axis. A pixel whose pick is a ring box
  reads its cell off the box. Refused, it walks again as today.
- **The order.**
  - The face pass now runs before the draw, so the `sraster` span includes
    it. The table gives `face` too.
  - The draw counts its own instances in args of its own, since the face
    pass may not write the args it is dispatched by.
  - The face pass binds 8 storage buffers, WebGPU's floor.

Timed in one Chrome tab at dpr 2, a 2056×1113 CSS viewport, a fixed render
scale of 1.00 and sky 3 p.m., with E2's motion and views. Each page was
loaded fresh, given one warm-up run, then two timed runs, then a run with
ray_counts.

- The two ports' walkers started at the same place this time, before the
  water patch.
- The E4 horizon runs with shadows off counted rays for part of their
  frames (the checkbox had stayed on). They are given as timed. A later
  run with it off matched them.
- A first E4 build bound one buffer twice for the sun's boxes. Its
  shadows-on runs were invalid and are not used.

## What it says

- **The ring does what it is for.** More pixels take a cell off a box, 65%
  against 59% at the horizon. A third as many walk again (0.06% against
  0.18%), and the miss lane's nodes fall by 42%. The beam and the samples
  are untouched, as the prune is.
- **It costs boxes.** About eleven times HEAD's a cast at the horizon and
  nine times looking down. The raster's span less the face pass goes from
  about 1.2 to 2.0 at the horizon, and from 0.9 to 2.3 looking down.
- **The bar.**
  - At the horizon with shadows on, the p95 interval holds (25–27 against
    26), though more frames are missed.
  - With shadows off, it rises from 18 to 20–22.
  - Looking down with shadows on, it rises from 18 to 23.
  - So E4 as built is over today's p95, by the raster's added boxes alone.
- **What would bring it under.** The ring's boxes are most of the draw.
  Many duplicate a box another sample stopped at, or a ring cell of the
  sample beside it. Fewer boxes, with no slant-dependent rule, means
  deduplicating:
  - ring boxes against the samples' own;
  - each ring cell drawn by one sample of those about it, as `alike` does
    for the samples' own.
- **Left for the user.** How much of the rows is left is the user's to
  look at, on :5178 against :5176.

## Second run: the boxes deduplicated

The same export with a dedupe, as the observed pyramid keys its nodes, timed
the same way in the same tab and viewport.

- **The key** is the box's cell and axis: zoom and axis in one word, then
  the cell's index. The axis is part of it because two boxes of one cell
  drawn across different axes are different faces. A lack's box takes axis
  3.
- **The claim** (pxl-shader raster.rs `claimed`).
  - Before a box is emitted, its key is claimed in a frame-tagged hash set
    with an atomic compare-exchange. Only the claimer emits, and a key
    already written this frame is skipped.
  - The samples' own boxes claim first (`sample_box_cs`), then the ring's
    (`face_cs`). So a ring cell that is some sample's own stop drops out,
    as does one another sample's ring already emitted.
  - Where a slot is claimed but not yet written, or past 8 probes, the box
    is emitted anyway. A duplicate draws the same pixels.
  - Which claim wins may vary, but the boxes of one key are one box, so the
    set drawn and the image do not.
- **The set** has 2^16 slots of 5 words, above the 28.6 k boxes the
  horizon emitted without it. It sits after the draw's own args in their
  buffer: the face pass is at WebGPU's 8 storage buffers, so it has no
  binding to spare. The CPU writes the frame's tag there each frame, so
  nothing is cleared.
- **No workgroup stage.** `face_cs` runs 64 boxes a workgroup, not a grid
  cell's samples, so a shared-memory stage would need its own key table
  and barriers. It was not built.
